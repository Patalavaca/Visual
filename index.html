Why weight loss helps your ME

Fat tissue physically compresses lymphatic vessels — the drainage network your Perrin treatment is trying to clear. Every stone lost reduces that compression and improves drainage capacity directly.

The thoracic duct, your primary lymphatic drain, runs through the torso. Abdominal fat puts direct mechanical pressure on it. Less weight means better flow — the two goals are biologically the same goal.

Weight loss also reduces the SNS load on your system. Adipose tissue produces inflammatory cytokines that keep the sympathetic nervous system in a low-level activated state. Reducing that load gives your nervous system a quieter baseline to recover from.
